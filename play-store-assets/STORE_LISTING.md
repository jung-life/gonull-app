## Full description (max 4000)
```
GoNull is an app blocker for people who've tried everything else.

Screen Time limits, app timers, willpower — if any of that worked, you wouldn't be reaching for your phone right now. GoNull is different. Instead of a flimsy reminder you dismiss in half a second, it puts real friction between you and the apps that eat your day.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose the apps that pull you in — social media, short-form video, games, shopping, news.
• When you open one, GoNull intercepts it with a block screen instead of an endless feed.
• Want in anyway? You commit to a time delay first. The countdown runs while you go live your life — no instant gratification.
• Every bypass gets progressively harder, so the easy path is the focused one.

WHY IT WORKS
Your rational brain needs about 20 seconds to override an impulse. GoNull buys you that time. The block screen asks what you're feeling and how strong the urge is — turning autopilot into a choice. Most of the time, the urge passes and you move on.

FEATURES
• App blocking with custom time-delayed unlocks
• Progressive friction — each unlock costs more than the last
• Focus Modes: Gym, Meditation, and Analog (2005-phone) mode that block everything except the essentials
• Real-World Quests — small offline actions to break the loop
• Boredom Training to rebuild your attention span
• Daily intentions and a Reclaim Journal to track what you get back
• Usage insights so you can see the time you've saved
• Accountability partner support

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. GoNull does not sell your data, show ads, or send your activity anywhere. The only thing it ever transmits is an anonymous crash report to help us fix bugs. Read our policy at gonull.app/privacy.

GoNull uses Android's Accessibility Service for one purpose: to detect when you open a blocked app so it can show the block screen. It never reads your screen content, your messages, or what you type.

Stop scrolling on autopilot. Put your phone back to work for you.

Download GoNull and break the loop.
```
